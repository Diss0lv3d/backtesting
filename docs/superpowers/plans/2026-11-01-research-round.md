# Research Round Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Every 5 proposer trials, `lab propose` runs a research round: one tool-less model call that reads the record and returns up to 3 new idea cards, a stop list and a summary, which the loop validates and writes into the record.

**Architecture:** A new typed contract (`ResearchNote`) beside `Proposal`; a `research` table and origin `research` in the record; a research instruction and a stop-list section in the prompt; a scheduler and a research round in the loop that reuse the proposal caller, fix-up mechanism and failure handling; the digest, the Ideas page and `lab ideas` show the results.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.12, SQLAlchemy (SQLite), Streamlit, typer, pytest. No new dependency.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-01-research-round-design.md` (binding); the proposer spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-proposer-design.md` for everything it doesn't change.

## Global Constraints

- The research call is the proposer's call: `model.call_with_retries` with the same argv (`--tools ""`), environment and init check. No web, no file access, no new subprocess.
- The research prompt = the static context (system) + `prompt.render_record(...)` + the fixed research instruction (user). Nothing else.
- The research schema has no `oneOf`/`allOf`/`anyOf` at its top level (the API refuses them; Ruling R42 of the proposer build).
- Research ideas pass `human.check` (the human-card rules) and are added all-or-none with origin `research`.
- `perp_lab/proposer/` imports none of `perp_lab.judge`, `perp_lab.holdout`, `perp_lab.store`, `perp_lab.data`, and starts no process except through `proc` (tests/test_proposer_boundaries.py).
- Nothing time-bearing reaches the model (research rows' times stay out of every prompt).
- A research failure never stops a session, except `ModelConfigError` (stop `model_config`).
- record.db exists on the user's machine: new tables come from `create_all`; new columns need the migration in `Record.__init__`.
- Config defaults: `research_every: 5` (0 = off), `research_max_ideas: 3`, `research_open_cap: 6`.
- Style: match the surrounding code; TDD (each test seen failing first); one concern per commit; stage files by path; commit trailers per the dispatch.

## Review Focus

1. A record whose research rounds keep failing (invalid replies): the session goes on, the next round is due after 5 more trials, no tight loop of research calls.
2. The "nothing to work on" trigger: runs at most once per `trials_used()` value — never two research calls in a row with no trial between.
3. The stop list reaching the proposer: present in every proposal prompt after an `ok` round, replaced (not accumulated) by the next `ok` round, absent before any.
4. The cap: with 6 untried open research ideas, a round adds none and records why; human and seed ideas don't count toward the cap.
5. The end-to-end sentinel test covers a research round's argv and stdin.

### Task 1: Settings and the research contract

**Files:** Modify `perp_lab/proposer/settings.py`, `config/lab.yaml`, `perp_lab/proposer/contract.py`. Create `perp_lab/proposer/research.py`. Test: `tests/test_settings.py`, `tests/test_contract.py`, `tests/test_research.py`.

**Interfaces:**
- `ProposerConfig` gains `research_every: int` (≥ 0), `research_max_ideas: int` (≥ 1), `research_open_cap: int` (≥ 1); `settings.load` validates them (StartRefused on a bad value, like the other fields).
- `contract.ResearchIdea` (the `IdeaCard` fields + `timeframe: str` + `builds_on: str`) and `contract.ResearchNote` (`ideas: tuple[ResearchIdea, ...]`, `stop: tuple[str, ...]`, `summary: str`), with field descriptions in the contract's `DOCS` style.
- `contract.research_schema(timeframes, max_ideas) -> dict`, `contract.describe_research(timeframes, max_ideas) -> str`, `contract.parse_research(obj, timeframes, max_ideas) -> ResearchNote` (raises `ContractError(errors)`), generated by the same machinery as `schema`/`describe`/`parse`. Limits: ideas 0..max_ideas; stop 0..5 items, each one line, ≤ 200 chars; summary ≤ 2,000 chars; `builds_on` one line ≤ 300 chars.
- `research.check_note(note: ResearchNote, record, pc) -> list[str]`: every idea through `human.check` (as a human card with its timeframe), plus titles distinct within the note; errors prefixed `idea #k (title): `.

### Task 3: The research round in the loop

**Files:** Modify `perp_lab/proposer/prompt.py`, `perp_lab/proposer/loop.py`, `perp_lab/proposer/digest.py`. Test: `tests/test_prompt.py`, `tests/test_loop.py`, `tests/test_digest.py`.

**Interfaces:**
- `prompt.research_instruction(pc) -> str`: fixed text per the spec's "The call" (purpose, redundancy, neutral exits, no numbers from the record, limits) followed by `contract.describe_research(...)`. No record content, no times.
- `prompt.stop_section(stop: list[str]) -> str`: `"Directions the research step recommends avoiding:\n- …"`, or `""` for an empty list.
- The proposal round's user prompt becomes `render_record(...) + stop_section(latest ok research's stop) + instruction(next_up)` (keep the existing order of record then instruction; the section sits between them).
- `Digest.research(added_ids, stop_count, outcome, summary)` writes one line (`research: +3 ideas (#17, #18, #19); stop list 4 items` / `research: invalid after fix-ups` / `research: error model_call`) and the summary indented under it.
- In `_Session`: `_research_due() -> bool` per the spec (every `research_every` trials since the last research row's `trials_used`; or nothing answerable, at most once per `trials_used()`; never when `research_every == 0`); `_research()` runs the round: the call through the same `_call` path (usage limits, model errors, token accounting with seq None, transcript at `state/transcripts/research-<id>.jsonl`), fix-ups on `ContractError` or `check_note` errors (up to `fixups_per_proposal`, with `prompt.fixup`), then writes ideas (or `capped`), the research row and the digest line. `run()` calls `_research()` before a proposal round when due, after the same quota/token waits.
- Failures per the spec: `ModelConfigError` → stop `model_config`; `ModelCallError` after retries or invalid after fix-ups → row with outcome, digest line, continue.

- [ ] **Step 1: failing tests** (fake Deps, fake clock, as tests/test_loop.py does): a research round after 5 trials (not before); `research_every: 0` never researches; the nothing-answerable trigger runs once and not again until a trial is spent; an `ok` round adds the ideas with origin research and the next proposal prompt carries the stop section; a second `ok` round replaces the stop list; the cap adds nothing and records `capped` (stop list still updated); invalid → fix-up → valid; invalid after all fix-ups → `invalid`, session continues; ModelCallError → `error: model_call`, session continues; ModelConfigError → stop `model_config`; a research call's tokens count toward the day's caps; no research row time and no `at` appear in any prompt (extend the no-times test); the digest line.
- [ ] **Step 2–4: fail, implement, pass; full suite.**
- [ ] **Step 5: commits** — `feat(proposer): the research instruction and the stop list in the proposal prompt`, `feat(proposer): a research round every N trials`.
